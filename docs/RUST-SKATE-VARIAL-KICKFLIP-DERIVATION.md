# Varial Kickflip shared-source derivation

`varial-kickflip-derived-tre-v1` is an authored derivation from the existing
Tre body reference. It is not a filmed Varial capture. Its exact normal
signature is Ollie, no switch/tail-first/body turn, flip +360 and shove -180.

The authoring input retains the shared body reference and phase timing.
The Varial-specific free feet collect closer to the deck; its pelvis follows
into a compact tuck and bent catch. Its own upper frame and bent-elbow balance
use a stable toe-side forward fold, then relaxed hands through Catch. This
replaces the rejected arched/high-arm Air and straight T-arm Catch. It is an
authored adaptation, not a filmed Varial body or a shared Tre change.
The board has its own smaller backside scoop and complete longitudinal flip.
The rear free-foot depth is smaller, and the final anatomical foot targets
retain their world sides while their deck-local X/Z and toe headings reverse.
The free-foot return retains its incoming velocity instead of freezing at the
start of the catch blend. Pelvis fitting uses the existing measured-leg reach
share; free-foot collection does not move grounded contacts or increase any
limb bounds.

The exact ID, movement kind and signature must all match. Other captures do
not opt in. The route uses its own Pop/release/free-Air/Catch/Land metadata,
the existing physical flight clock and the shared Tre-body source408 root
join. An actually observed flatground takeoff plane is required. The existing
full-board support correction is bounded to0.12m and moves body/board together;
missing plane, excess correction, hand ownership and measured reach still
refuse capture. Physics, scoring and grounded trick knowledge are unchanged.

The source baker performs strict encoding before output mutation. Both
temporary payloads are written before either target is replaced, preserving
the previous reader-valid pack on parse, validation or staging failure.

Normal/low and both anatomical previews need independent mesh review. The
reference remains labelled Tre and is never mirrored or represented as filmed
Varial board motion. Offline reconstruction review, owner pack approval and
native Shadow validation remain separate gates. Source output is unreviewed.

The current private replays use actual Pad input and native Step/Land records,
then the measured body route. Normal charge is .30 s and lower charge .12 s;
the lower frozen flight is about .556 s, rather than the old synthetic .528 s
profile. All four routes reach the native Varial landing and retain capture
through Air. This is managed controlled-geometry evidence, not an engine or
Shadow playtest.
