# Sound source and license

These 54 WAV assets and the accompanying Demo.wav were authored from original deterministic procedural synthesis for this project. The generator's seeded random excitations, modal wood/steel/rubber/cloth resonators, dry friction noise, irregular impacts and envelopes are the complete sound sources. No recorded game audio, samples from commercial sound packs, external sound files, speech, recordings of people or copyrighted music were used. There is no separate flip sound and no room/reverb convolution.

The exact generator is supplied as `../generate_skate_sounds.py`, with dependency versions in `../requirements.txt`. Each variant's seed and the generator SHA-256 are recorded in manifest.json. The noise/mode parameters are original authored approximations of materials; this is not a claim of real skateboard field recordings.
